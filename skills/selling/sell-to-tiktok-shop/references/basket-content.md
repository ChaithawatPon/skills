# Compliant basket-content workflow

Use this workflow for TikTok Shop videos that attach a product basket.

1. Confirm the exact live product and factual claims that the listing supports.
   Regulated-product claims require current platform and legal evidence.
2. Build only from media the user owns or has a license to reuse. Record the
   source and license for stock footage, music, fonts, and third-party assets.
3. Use AI for original scripting, shot lists, captions, voice, backgrounds, or
   transformations only when the input rights and tool terms allow commercial
   use. AI output does not make copied creator footage safe to repost.
4. Make an original demonstration: show the real item, one problem, one useful
   feature, and one truthful outcome. Keep price, availability, and promotion
   claims synchronized with the live listing.
5. Review the final frame-by-frame output for private data, false before/after
   results, unsupported health or income claims, copyrighted clips, misleading
   edits, and missing ad or affiliate disclosure.
6. Preview the selected product basket and caption. Request exact approval for
   that post and product before publishing.
7. After publication, verify the public post, basket link, disclosure, and
   product availability. Record the canonical post URL; stop if any element is
   missing or under review.

Reject downloaded creator videos, cropped watermarks, recreated copyrighted
ads, unlicensed music, fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, and claims not
proved by the exact product.
