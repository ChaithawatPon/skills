# Platform Voice and Tone Playbook

Use this reference to adapt post voice, formatting, and tone to the specific culture and audience of each supported platform. A cross-platform campaign must never reuse identical phrasing across platforms; adapt the tone while preserving truthful core facts.

---

## 1. LinkedIn (Professional Thought Leadership)
- **Audience**: Engineers, founders, recruiters, industry peers.
- **Tone**: Professional, analytical, growth-oriented, substantive. No generic corporate buzzwords.
- **Structure**:
  - Hook: Strong architectural observation or lesson learned.
  - Problem & Context: Real-world constraint or technical challenge.
  - Solution / Workflow: Concrete user-visible approach and design decisions.
  - Takeaway: Actionable takeaway or strategic insight.
  - CTA: Thoughtful open question prompting professional discussion.
- **Formatting**: Short 1-2 sentence paragraphs, clean bullet points, document slides (PDF carousel) for deep dives.

---

## 2. X / Twitter (Punchy, Hook-Driven & Dense)
- **Audience**: Devs, indie hackers, tech builders.
- **Tone**: Fast, confident, punchy, insight-dense.
- **Structure**:
  - Standalone post: Bold thesis or surprising result + key takeaway + link/media.
  - Thread (1/N):
    - Slide 1: High-stakes hook + promise of what’s inside.
    - Middle: Step-by-step breakdown (one distinct lesson per post).
    - Final: Summary takeaway + bookmark/repost CTA.
- **Formatting**: Maximum whitespace, minimal filler, concise code or architecture snippets.

---

## 3. Threads (Conversational, Relatable & Casual)
- **Audience**: Creative builders, casual tech community, day-to-day thinkers.
- **Tone**: Conversational, humble, witty, spontaneous, relatable. Feels like a live thought bubble.
- **Structure**:
  - Conversational hook ("Spent 3 hours debugging X, turns out...").
  - Vulnerable or relatable realization.
  - Open prompt inviting peers to share their experiences.
- **Formatting**: Under 500 characters, lowercase or relaxed typography, natural speech rhythm.

---

## 4. Facebook (Narrative Storytelling & Community Peer Sharing)
- **Page / Profile**:
  - **Tone**: Approachable, warm, educational narrative.
  - **Structure**: Narrative storytelling connecting the user's journey to the project outcome. Contact information and polite closing footer.
- **Facebook Groups**:
  - **Tone**: Community-first, peer-to-peer sharing. Absolutely no spammy broadcast tone.
  - **Structure**:
    - Humble introduction.
    - Explicit framing: Sharing a solution or asking for feedback.
    - Respect each group's promotion policies.

---

## 5. Instagram (Visual-First & Engaging Story)
- **Audience**: Visual learners, younger creators, peers.
- **Tone**: Aesthetic, energetic, inspiring, visually supportive.
- **Structure**:
  - Hook in line 1 (visible before "more").
  - Body: Scannable 3-5 bullet highlights matching the carousel slides or Reel visuals.
  - CTA: "Save for later" or "Tag someone who needs this".
  - Tags: 5-10 clean tags placed at the bottom.
- **Formatting**: Line breaks between thoughts, aesthetic emojis as bullet anchors.

---

## 6. TikTok (Fast-Paced, Hook-First & Value-Packed)
- **Audience**: Fast-scrolling mobile viewers seeking quick answers.
- **Tone**: Direct, energetic, punchy conversational phrasing.
- **Structure**:
  - 0–3s visual & audio hook answering "Why should I care right now?".
  - 3–15s rapid problem-solution walkthrough.
  - 15–30s key result or demo.
  - CTA: Follow / save / drop a question.
- **Formatting**: Short caption, high-relevance hashtags, companion on-screen text.

---

## 7. Lemon8 (Curated, Warm & Value-Dense Guide)
- **Audience**: Productivity seekers, students, lifestyle and tech tool enthusiasts.
- **Tone**: Warm, helpful, friendly curated guide ("Top tips...", "Curated tools...", "Step-by-step guide...").
- **Structure**:
  - Slide 1 (Cover): Bold title sticker / visual hook.
  - Slide 2–N: High-contrast 3:4 cards, numbered step-by-step guides.
  - Caption: Clean bullet points with emoji categorizers + CTA to save.
  - Tags: 5–10 niche search tags (`#Productivity #TechHacks`).

---

## 8. RedNote / Xiaohongshu (Aesthetic, Highly Structured & Practical)
- **Audience**: Visual learners, meticulous tool and aesthetic workflow curators.
- **Tone**: Highly structured, polite, detail-oriented, generous with practical tips.
- **Structure**:
  - Catchy title with emojis.
  - Structured sections (e.g. 📌 Why use this / 🛠 Setup steps / 💡 Pro-tip).
  - High-utility takeaways and keyword-rich tags for search discoverability.
- **Formatting**: 3:4 vertical cards, distinct visual hierarchy, clean bullet points.

---

## 9. Reddit (Authentic, Technical & Builder Transparency)
- **Audience**: Skeptical, technical, community-governed subreddits (e.g., r/webdev, r/SideProject, r/selfhosted).
- **Tone**: Authentic, honest, humble, technically transparent. **Zero corporate marketing or sales pitch**.
- **Structure**:
  - Descriptive, non-clickbait title stating what it is and what stack/method was used.
  - The "Why": Personal itch, problem encountered, or experiment context.
  - The "How": Technical architecture, trade-offs, libraries chosen.
  - "What Failed": Honest discussion of bugs, mistakes, or performance bottlenecks.
  - Open request for code review, feedback, or criticism.
- **Formatting**: Clean GitHub-flavored Markdown, headers, code blocks, bullet points.

---

## 10. YouTube (Contextual, Community & Educational)
- **Audience**: Dedicated subscribers and searchers looking for in-depth mastery.
- **Tone**: Educational, respectful, clear, structured.
- **Structure**:
  - Community image post: High-contrast 1:1 or 16:9 infographic or poll with context paragraph.
  - Video / Shorts announcement: Clear overview of what’s covered, chapter timestamps, and resource links.
