# Do Assignment

Start from a verified brief collected by `$check-assignment`. Record course, title, source, due date, rubric, deliverable type, formatting rules, and every requirement. Missing facts stay `Not stated`.

## Ideas first

Ask one question at a time, maximum five questions total. Stop early when answers cover the student's opinion, personal examples, preferred conclusion, tone, and exclusions. Never invent an answer or personal experience.

Show a complete requirement checklist and outline. Wait for approval of that exact outline before creating the deliverable.

## Visual prototype

For presentations, posters, and visual deliverables, after outline approval build 2–3 clearly different throwaway directions with representative content. Wait for the student to choose one direction before building the full deliverable. Skip this branch for ordinary reports, worksheets, and quizzes unless it answers a real design question.

## Build and review

Create the requested slides, report, worksheet, activity, template, or draft quiz answers in the student's chosen tone. Match every checklist item and keep claims source-backed.

Show the exact draft and checklist. Stop for review. Upload, email, turn-in, quiz submission, or any other submission requires fresh approval for the exact file, destination, and action. Any edit after approval resets that gate.
