# Find Room

Find a small evidence-backed rental shortlist. Keep saved searches, private group URLs, account state, contact details, and notification destinations in local config outside Git.

## Search contract

1. Resolve monthly budget, location, move date, property type, commute destination and limit, lease length, occupants, and deal breakers. Never widen them silently.
2. Search current listing portals, visible social listings, property pages, and maps. Collection pages are leads; open the individual listing before ranking it.
3. Verify numeric monthly rent, deposit and advance payment, recurring fees, availability date, address, property identity, listing freshness, room facts, and direct URL. Exclude sales, nightly stays, wanted posts, deposits presented as rent, and out-of-scope locations.
4. Verify the commute with a current route. Label candidates `manual_review` when a required route or fact cannot be verified.
5. Rank up to five candidates by requirement fit, total move-in cost, commute, evidence quality, safety signals, and practical amenities.

## Evidence and outreach

Capture tight room/building images and route evidence without account UI, messages, notifications, or unrelated content. Treat hidden fees, inconsistent addresses, implausible prices, advance transfers, unclear ownership, and stock-photo-only pages as risks.

Research and drafting are read-only. Before contacting a landlord or submitting a form, show the exact property, recipient, channel, message, personal data, and attachment. Send only after explicit approval of that action in the current session. Stop immediately on requests for payment, identity documents, recovery codes, or sensitive account data.

Report direct links, observed time, verified costs, commute evidence, missing facts, risks, and one next action.
