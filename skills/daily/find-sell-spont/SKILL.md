# Find Sell Spot

Find a small trial stall for personal closet clearance, not a wholesale business or permanent lease.

1. Resolve location, dates, products, maximum total fee, indoor/outdoor preference, and travel limits. Default to events within the next eight weeks when dates are omitted.
2. Search current organizer, venue, market, booking-form, and social-post sources. Directories and articles are leads only.
3. Keep candidates only when current evidence proves the venue, event date or schedule, allowed product type, fee or `fee unknown`, booking status, and a working application path.
4. Return at most five options ranked by total trial cost, seller fit, travel convenience, included equipment, weather protection, and urgency. Put one recommendation first with its full one-day cost and exact next action.

For each option, report dates, hours, deadline, fees and deposits, equipment, seller eligibility, stall rules, organizer route, source URL, observed time, and missing evidence. Capture a tight screenshot of the dated fee/application facts without personal feeds or account UI.

Research and drafting are read-only. Before any message, form submission, booking, document upload, or payment, show the exact event, recipient, text, requested personal data, fee, and cancellation terms; execute only after fresh approval.
